1. Overview

KyroMed (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates a hyperlocal medicine and health supplement commerce mobile application in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, store and protect your personal information, in line with Google Play policies and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

2. Information We Collect

When you use KyroMed, we collect only the data necessary to provide shopping, fast local delivery, user reviews and seller storefront features:

  • Account information (Google Sign-In): your name, email address and unique user identifier provided when you sign in using Google Authentication.
  • Delivery & address details: your 6-digit Indian pincode, city and street/delivery address saved to your profile and orders.
  • Location data (optional, foreground only): if you tap “Use Live GPS Location”, we request foreground location permission (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) solely to detect your current Indian delivery pincode and address. We never track your location in the background.
  • Orders, cart & reviews: items in your shopping bag, order history, the selected payment method label (UPI, Card or Cash on Delivery), and product reviews and ratings you publish. KyroMed does not store raw bank account numbers, card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Seller listings (optional): product names, pricing, stock and order fulfilment updates if you list items in the Seller Studio.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Process and track hyperlocal medicine and supplement orders.
  • Determine serviceability and express delivery times for your Indian pincode.
  • Display verified product reviews and ratings to other shoppers.
  • Enable sellers to manage inventory and fulfil orders.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Who We Share Information With

  • Sellers and delivery partners: to fulfil an order, we share the details needed for that order (such as your name, delivery address and the items ordered) with the seller and any delivery partner handling it. They may use this only to complete and support your order.
  • Service providers: we use Google services (Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore) to run the app. Google processes data on our behalf under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by Indian law, a court order or a lawful request from authorities.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and strict server-side security rules that restrict access to authorized users.

6. How Long We Keep Data

We keep your account and profile data while your account is active. When you request deletion, we delete or anonymise your data as described below. Order records that Indian tax or regulatory law requires us to keep may be retained only for the legally required period.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, correct or request deletion of your account and personal data at any time, to withdraw your consent, and to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDPA.

  • You can update your delivery pincode, city and address directly inside the app.
  • To request permanent deletion of your KyroMed account, profile, cart and personal data, use our Account Deletion page, or email sharan1697@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “KyroMed Account Deletion Request”. Requests are processed within 7 business days. Order records required for tax or regulatory compliance under Indian law may be retained only as legally mandated.

8. Children’s Privacy

KyroMed is intended for users aged 18 and above for purchasing medicines and health supplements. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
