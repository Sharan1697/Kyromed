# Privacy Policy for KyroMed **Effective Date:** [November 16, 2026] **Developer / Entity:** [Kyromed] **Contact Email:** sharan1697@gmail.com **Region:** India ## 1. Overview KyroMed ("we", "our", or "us") operates a hyperlocal medicine and health supplement commerce mobile application in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in compliance with Google Play policies and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). ## 2. Information We Collect When you use KyroMed, we collect only the data necessary to provide shopping, fast local delivery, user reviews, and seller storefront features: - **Account Information (Google Sign-In):** Your name, email address, and unique user identifier provided when you sign in using Google Authentication. - **Delivery & Address Details:** Your 6-digit Indian pincode, city, and street/delivery address saved to your profile and orders. - **Location Data (Optional, Foreground Only):** If you tap "Use Live GPS Location," we request foreground location permission (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) solely to detect your current Indian delivery pincode and address. We never track your location in the background. - **Orders, Cart & Reviews:** Items in your shopping bag, order history, selected payment method label (UPI, Card, or Cash on Delivery), and product reviews/ratings you publish. KyroMed does not store raw bank account numbers, card numbers, or UPI PINs. - **Seller Listings (Optional):** Product names, pricing, stock, and order fulfillment updates if you list items in the Seller Studio. ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use your information strictly to: - Authenticate your account securely via Firebase Authentication. - Process and track hyperlocal medicine and supplement orders. - Determine serviceability and express delivery times for your Indian pincode. - Display verified product reviews and ratings to other shoppers. - Enable sellers to manage inventory and fulfill orders. We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties, and we do **not** use third-party advertising SDKs. ## 4. Data Storage & Security Your data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and strict server-side security rules restricting access to authorized users. ## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your account and personal data at any time: - You can update your delivery pincode, city, and address directly inside the app. - To request permanent deletion of your KyroMed account, profile, cart, and personal data, email **sharan1697@gmail.com** from your registered email address with the subject **"KyroMed Account Deletion Request"**. Requests are processed within 7 business days (order records required for tax/regulatory compliance under Indian law may be retained only as legally mandated). ## 6. Children's Privacy KyroMed is intended for users aged 18 and above for purchasing medicines and health supplements. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. ## 7. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
