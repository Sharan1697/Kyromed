Delete Your KyroMed Account

App: KyroMed · Developer: Sai Sharan
Last updated: November 16, 2026

You can ask us to permanently delete your KyroMed account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open your email app and sign in with the same email address you use to sign in to KyroMed.
  2. Send an email to sharan1697@gmail.com with the subject KyroMed Account Deletion Request. (The link opens a ready-made email.)
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email address and user identifier).
  • Your saved delivery addresses, pincode and city.
  • Your shopping cart and order history shown in the app.
  • Your seller listings, if you used the Seller Studio.
  • Your reviews and ratings are removed or anonymised.

What we may keep

Order and transaction records that Indian tax or regulatory law requires us to keep may be retained only for the legally required period, and are not used for any other purpose.
